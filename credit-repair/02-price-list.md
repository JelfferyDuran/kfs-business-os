# 02 — KFS Per-Deletion Price List

**Kingdom Financial Services — presented by Noel Duran**

KFS's fee schedule for credit repair, adapted from the SOYDAAT course price list (shared Oct 23, 2026) and restated as KFS's own schedule. Fees are charged **per verified result** — see the billing trigger that applies to every line.

> **Billing trigger (applies to every line below):** the fee is earned **only when the deletion or correction is verified on that bureau's updated report**. Nothing is charged up front (CROA §1679b(b)); nothing is charged for an item that remains. See `01-compliance-and-billing.md`.

## Fee Schedule

| Service | KFS fee | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Personal information correction (name/address/employer variants) | $50 – $150 | per bureau |
| Hard inquiry removal | $50 – $150 | per inquiry |
| Collection removal | $200 – $500 | per collection, per bureau |
| Late payment removal/correction | $150 – $300 | per late payment, per bureau |
| Charge-off removal/correction | $300 – $500 | per account, per bureau |
| Student loan tradeline correction/removal | $300 – $500 | per account, per bureau |
| Child support reporting case | $3,000 – $5,000 | per case |
| Bankruptcy reporting case | $3,000 – $5,000 | per case |
| Vehicle repossession reporting case | $1,000 – $2,000 | per case |

Where a range is shown, the exact fee within the range is set in the client's written contract before work begins, based on the item's complexity — and is still only billed after the verified result.

## Worked Example — A Typical Case

A mid-size file: **1 collection** reporting on all 3 bureaus, **1 charge-off** on all 3 bureaus, and **2 hard inquiries**.

| Item | Math | Subtotal |
|---|---|---|
| Collection × 3 bureaus | $200–$500 × 3 | $600 – $1,500 |
| Charge-off × 3 bureaus | $300–$500 × 3 | $900 – $1,500 |
| Hard inquiries × 2 | $50–$150 × 2 | $100 – $300 |
| **Total if every item is removed** | | **≈ $1,600 – $3,300** |

For comparison, a flat monthly model at $99/month over ~5 months totals ≈ $495 — the per-deletion model prices the *result*, not the time. Either way, billing follows performance: per-deletion fees post only on verified deletions; a monthly fee (if ever used) is billed in arrears only.

## Cautions

- **Accurate items are never sold as removable.** Several categories above — child support, bankruptcy, student loans — are often *accurate* public-record or furnisher items. KFS takes these cases only to dispute what is **inaccurate, unverifiable, incomplete, or obsolete** (wrong dates, wrong balances, items reporting past the legal period, mixed-file errors). No client is ever promised that an accurate item will come off.
- **Case-priced categories** (child support, bankruptcy, repossession) are quoted per case in writing before work starts, and billed per the same rule: on verified results, per the contract's milestones.
- Fees, units, and triggers must match the signed contract exactly. If the contract and this schedule ever disagree, the signed contract governs — and the schedule gets fixed.
