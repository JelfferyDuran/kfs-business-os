# 03 — Client Intake Checklist (SOP)

**Kingdom Financial Services — presented by Noel Duran**

One checklist per client. The client file lives in the client's own secured folder — **never in this repo, never loose in chat** — so nothing gets lost and no client data sits in the wrong place.

---

## Step 1 — Compliance gate (all required before signing)

- [ ] CROA-compliant service contract signed — written contract **before** any service
- [ ] Federal "Consumer Credit File Rights Under State and Federal Law" disclosure given; signed copy in file
- [ ] Cancellation notice provided — client has **3 business days** to cancel, no questions asked
- [ ] Written authorization signed: client authorizes KFS to obtain their credit reports and dispute on their behalf
- [ ] **NO payment collected** — CROA: fees only **after** the work is performed and verified (per-deletion milestones per `02-price-list.md`; monthly only in arrears). No setup fees, no deposits.
- [ ] **NO outcome promises made** — no guaranteed deletions, scores, or timelines (illegal, and it voids trust)

## Step 2 — Client information to collect

- [ ] Full legal name (as on ID)
- [ ] Date of birth (needed for bureau letters)
- [ ] Current address + addresses for the last 2 years (letters require them)
- [ ] Phone + email
- [ ] How they found KFS / referral source

## Step 3 — Documents (store ONLY in the client folder)

- [ ] Government photo ID (copy)
- [ ] SSN proof (card or document showing full number — bureaus require it for identity verification)
- [ ] Proof of address (utility bill / bank statement)
- [ ] 3-bureau credit reports (client pulls at AnnualCreditReport.com, or via Credit Hero Score invite)

> ⚠️ Do **not** have the client send SSN/ID documents over regular chat before the file is open. Scheduling and explanation over WhatsApp is fine; documents move through the intake step, in person where possible (see the TSR note in `01-compliance-and-billing.md`).

## Step 4 — Case setup (after signing)

- [ ] Baseline recorded: scores + full negative-item inventory from all 3 bureaus
- [ ] Monitoring live (Credit Hero Score) + review cadence set (monthly)
- [ ] Round 1 dispute plan drafted from the audit — every item logged (bureau, item, reason, date mailed)
- [ ] Copies of every letter sent given to the client (their right, and good practice)
- [ ] Case log started: one running file — dates, letters, responses, deletions, score snapshots

## Step 5 — Billing discipline

- [ ] Fee for each target item confirmed in the signed contract (within the `02-price-list.md` ranges)
- [ ] Invoice issued only when a deletion/correction is **verified on the bureau's updated report**
- [ ] Before/after reports filed with the invoice — every charge traces to a documented result

## Service lane (stay inside it)

- ✅ Credit repair: audits, dispute letters, bureau/creditor follow-up, credit education, monitoring
- ❌ Debt negotiation/settlement — in New Jersey that requires a debt-adjuster license. Do not offer it.
- ❌ Advising a client to create a new identity, use a CPN, or dispute accurate debts as "not mine" — prohibited under CROA.
