# Letter 08 — Method of Verification Request

**When to use:** The bureau answered a prior dispute with "verified as accurate" and the client maintains the item is wrong. Under §611(a)(6)(B)(iii) the bureau's results notice must include a statement of the right to request this description, and §611(a)(7) requires the bureau, on request, to provide a **description of the procedure** used to determine accuracy — including the furnisher contacted and its business name, address, and phone number. Send within days of the "verified" response.
**Uso:** Después de que el buró responde "verificado": se exige la descripción del procedimiento usado y los datos del furnisher (§611(a)(7)).

By notice dated {{bureau_response_date}}, you informed me the information was "verified" as accurate. Pursuant to §611(a)(7), I request that you provide me, within 15 days of this request, a description of the procedure used to determine the accuracy and completeness of the disputed information, including the business name, address, and telephone number of each furnisher contacted in connection with that reinvestigation.

I also ask you to confirm specifically: (1) whether the documents and evidence I submitted with my dispute were forwarded to the furnisher, as §611(a)(2) requires; and (2) whether the reinvestigation consisted of anything beyond an automated comparison of the furnisher's own records — the same records whose accuracy is in dispute.

I maintain that the item is inaccurate for the reasons stated in my original dispute. Upon receipt of your description, I will supplement my dispute with any additional evidence the procedure identifies. If the procedure cannot be described or did not in fact occur, the information cannot stand as verified and must be deleted under §611(a)(5).

Thank you for your compliance with this request.

**Closing checklist**
- [ ] Enclosures: government photo ID; proof of address; copy of the bureau's "verified" results notice
- [ ] Original dispute date and bureau response date filled from the case log — accuracy here is the whole letter
- [ ] Calendar the bureau's 15-day response duty under §611(a)(7)
- [ ] On response: if the furnisher is identified and the item is still wrong, escalate — direct furnisher dispute under §623(a)(8) and/or a new §611 dispute with fresh evidence
- [ ] Sent certified mail, return receipt requested; dates logged
- [ ] Copy given to the client
