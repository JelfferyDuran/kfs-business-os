# Letter 07 — Hard Inquiry Removal (No Permissible Purpose)

**When to use:** A hard inquiry appears from a company the client never applied with, never authorized to pull credit, and has no account or permissible purpose with. FCRA §604 limits who may obtain a report and for what purposes; §611 governs the dispute. Do **not** use for inquiries the client actually authorized (dealer rate-shopping, a real application) — those are accurate and stay.
**Uso:** Para consultas (inquiries) que el cliente nunca autorizó ni solicitó. No usar para consultas legítimas — esas son exactas y se quedan.

I have never applied for credit, employment, insurance, or any other service with this company; I have never authorized it to obtain my credit report; and I have no account or business relationship with it that would give it a permissible purpose under FCRA §604. {{optional_sentence: "I first learned of this inquiry on {{discovery_date}} when I reviewed my report."}}

Under §611, I request a reinvestigation of this inquiry. Please contact the inquirer and require it to document the permissible purpose and my authorization for this access. If it cannot produce my signed authorization or another lawful permissible purpose, the inquiry was made without authority and must be **deleted** from my file.

Please send me written notice of the results of your investigation and an updated copy of my report reflecting the outcome.

**Closing checklist**
- [ ] Enclosures: government photo ID; proof of address
- [ ] Client has confirmed, in the case notes, that they truly never applied or authorized — an authorized inquiry is accurate and is NOT disputed
- [ ] Consider a parallel letter to the inquiring company demanding proof of authorization and deletion (log it if sent)
- [ ] If the inquiry suggests identity theft: stop, and follow the identity-theft protocol (FTC report + §605B block) instead of this letter
- [ ] Sent certified mail, return receipt requested; mailing date and ~30-day deadline logged
- [ ] Copy given to the client
