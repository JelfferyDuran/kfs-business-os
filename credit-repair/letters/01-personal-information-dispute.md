# Letter 01 — Personal Information Dispute

**When to use:** The report contains personal identifiers that are not the client's — misspelled or foreign name variants, addresses the client never lived at, employers the client never worked for, or a wrong date of birth/SSN digit. Run this letter **first**, before any tradeline dispute: wrong identifiers are a leading cause of mixed files and misdirected investigations.
**Uso:** Cuando el reporte tiene nombres, direcciones o empleadores que no son del cliente. Se envía primero, antes de disputar cuentas.

**Re: Dispute of inaccurate personal information — request for reinvestigation and correction under FCRA §611 (15 U.S.C. §1681i)**

Dear Sir or Madam:

I am writing to dispute personal identifying information appearing on my credit report (report/confirmation number {{report_number}}, dated {{report_date}}). Under §611 of the Fair Credit Reporting Act, you are required to conduct a reasonable reinvestigation of disputed information and to maintain reasonable procedures to assure maximum possible accuracy under §605/§607(b). The following items are inaccurate and must be corrected or deleted:

1. **Name reported as "{{incorrect_name_variant}}"** — This is not my name. My legal name is **{{client_full_name}}**, as shown on the enclosed identification.
2. **Address reported as "{{incorrect_address}}"** — I have never lived at this address. My address history for the past {{years}} years is: {{correct_address_history}}.
3. **Employer reported as "{{incorrect_employer}}"** — I have never been employed by this company. My employer is {{correct_employer_or_none}}.

{{optional_mixed_file_sentence: "These identifiers do not belong to me and suggest my file may have been mixed with another consumer's file. Please also confirm that no accounts belonging to another consumer are reporting in my file."}}

Please correct my personal information to match the enclosed documentation, delete the identifiers listed above, and send me an updated copy of my report reflecting the corrections, as §611(a)(6) requires. If any item is verified as accurate, please describe the procedure used to make that determination.

**Closing checklist**
- [ ] Enclosures: copy of government photo ID; proof of current address (utility bill or bank statement)
- [ ] Report number and date confirmed from the actual report pull
- [ ] Every incorrect identifier quoted exactly as it appears on the report
- [ ] Sent certified mail, return receipt requested; mailing date and deadline (~30 days) logged in the case log
- [ ] Copy given to the client
