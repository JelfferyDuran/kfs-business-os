# Letter 04 — Debt Validation Request (to the Collector, FDCPA §809)

**When to use:** A collection agency is pursuing the client — by letter, call, or a tradeline — and the debt has not been validated. Addressed to the **collector**, not the bureau. Strongest within 30 days of the collector's first written notice (the §809 validation window), but the request may be sent whenever validation has never been provided.
**Uso:** Se envía al cobrador, no al buró, para exigir la validación de la deuda bajo FDCPA §809. La colección debe pausar hasta que validen.

{{client_city_state_zip}}

{{date}}

{{collection_agency_name}}
{{collection_agency_address}}

**Re: Request for validation of debt under FDCPA §809(b) (15 U.S.C. §1692g)**
**Account/Reference number (as stated in your notice):** {{collector_reference_number}}
**Original creditor (as stated):** {{original_creditor_name}}

Dear Sir or Madam:

I am in receipt of your communication dated {{collector_notice_date}} regarding the above-referenced alleged debt. I dispute this debt and request validation pursuant to §809 of the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act.

Please provide:

1. The amount of the debt, itemized — principal, interest, and every fee — and how each charge was calculated;
2. The name of the original creditor and, if different from your records, the current creditor;
3. Documentation bearing my signature or otherwise establishing that I am the person obligated on this account;
4. If you are not the original creditor, documentation establishing your authority to collect this debt, including the chain of assignment from the original creditor.

Under §809(b), until you obtain verification of the debt and mail it to me, you must **cease collection of the debt**, including reporting or continuing to report it as an unpaid collection to any credit bureau. I also request that all further communication with me regarding this matter be in writing.

This letter is not a refusal to pay any debt I lawfully owe; it is an exercise of my statutory right to verification before any collection proceeds. Please respond within the time the law provides.

Sincerely,

{{client_signature}}
{{client_full_name}}

---

**Closing checklist**
- [ ] Sent within 30 days of the collector's first written notice whenever possible (preserves the full §809(b) cease-collection protection)
- [ ] Collector's name/address and reference number copied exactly from their notice
- [ ] No ID or proof of address needed — this letter goes to the collector, not a bureau; do **not** enclose SSN documents
- [ ] Sent certified mail, return receipt requested; mailing date logged; any continued collection activity after mailing noted in the case log
- [ ] Copy given to the client
