# Letter 06 — Charge-Off Dispute

**When to use:** A charged-off account reports an inaccurate element — wrong balance after charge-off, a balance still growing as if open, wrong date of first delinquency, status shown as open/past due instead of charged off, the account was paid or settled but doesn't say so, or the account is not the client's. Dispute the **specific inaccuracy**, not the existence of a charge-off that is accurate.
**Uso:** Para errores en cuentas cargadas (charge-off): balance, estatus o fechas incorrectas. No se disputa un charge-off que es exacto.

**Closing checklist**
- [ ] Enclosures: government photo ID; proof of address; payment/settlement records or statements supporting the claimed correction
- [ ] Only the element that is actually wrong is disputed — balance, status, or dates, stated precisely
- [ ] Check the §605 clock: DOFD + 7 years (+180 days) — if the item is obsolete, dispute on that ground instead (Letter 02/03 wording)
- [ ] Sent certified mail, return receipt requested; mailing date and ~30-day deadline logged
- [ ] Copy given to the client
