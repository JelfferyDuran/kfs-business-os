# Letter 09 — Goodwill Adjustment Request (to the Original Creditor)

**When to use:** The late payment(s) are **accurate** — this is not a dispute and must never be disguised as one. Sent to the **original creditor** when the client has a genuinely good story: a long relationship, an isolated slip with a real cause (medical emergency, family crisis, a one-time autopay failure), and a strong payment record before and since. Creditors grant goodwill adjustments at their discretion; honesty and accountability are the entire strategy.
**Uso:** Para pagos tardes que SÍ son correctos, con buena historia y buen historial desde entonces. Es una petición amable al acreedor original — NO es una disputa.

**Closing checklist**
- [ ] Confirm in the case notes: the late payment IS accurate — if it is not, use Letter 05 (a real dispute) instead
- [ ] The explanation is true, brief, and takes responsibility — no blame-shifting, no invented hardship
- [ ] Payment record since the late mark verified from statements before the letter claims it
- [ ] Sent to the creditor's executive/customer-care address where possible; regular or certified mail both acceptable (no bureau clock involved)
- [ ] If declined: wait, keep the account current, and retry once after 6+ more months of on-time payments — do not escalate or threaten
- [ ] Copy given to the client
