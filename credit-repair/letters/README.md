# Letter Library — Index & Usage Rules

**Kingdom Financial Services — presented by Noel Duran**

Ten original dispute and request templates, written from FCRA/FDCPA first principles for KFS casework. Each letter has `{{placeholders}}` to complete from the client's file, a "When to use / Uso" note, and a closing checklist.

## Usage rules (every letter, every time)

1. **Letters to bureaus are written in English** — bureau processing is in English regardless of the client's preferred language.
2. **Send by certified mail, return receipt requested.** The green card and the mailing date start the clock and prove it.
3. **One dispute reason per item.** Each item gets its own specific, factual basis. Never stack every reason on every item.
4. **Attach ID + proof of address** to every bureau letter (copy of government photo ID and a utility bill or bank statement showing the client's name and current address).
5. **Log everything.** Record the bureau, item, reason, and mailing date in the case log; the bureau's investigation deadline is **~30 days** from receipt (FCRA §611). Calendar it.
6. **Never dispute accurate items as errors.** Every letter states facts that are true for that client and that item. A dispute with no factual basis is frivolous — it hurts the client and exposes KFS (CROA §1679b(a)).
7. **The client gets a copy** of every letter sent on their behalf.
8. **Placeholders only in this repo.** Fill in letters inside the client's own secured folder — a completed letter never comes back into this repository.

## The letters

| # | Letter | Send to | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Personal Information Dispute | Bureau | Wrong name/address/employer variants or identifiers on the report — always clean this up **first**, before tradeline disputes |
| 02 | General Inaccuracy Dispute (§611) | Bureau | An item-specific inaccuracy on a tradeline that doesn't fit a more specific letter below |
| 03 | Collection Dispute | Bureau | A collection tradeline that is inaccurate, unverifiable, not the client's, duplicated, or obsolete |
| 04 | Debt Validation Request (FDCPA §809) | **Collector** | A collector is pursuing a debt — demand validation; collection must pause until they verify |
| 05 | Late Payment Dispute | Bureau | A late payment reported for a month the client paid on time, or wrong late status/date |
| 06 | Charge-Off Dispute | Bureau | Balance, status, or date errors on a charged-off account |
| 07 | Hard Inquiry Removal | Bureau | An inquiry with no permissible purpose — the client never applied or authorized |
| 08 | Method of Verification Request | Bureau | Bureau answered "verified" — demand the description of the procedure used and the furnisher's contact info |
| 09 | Goodwill Adjustment Request | **Original creditor** | Accurate late payments with a good story and a strong payment record since — a request, **not** a dispute |
| 10 | Reinsertion Notice Violation | Bureau | A previously deleted item reappeared without the required written notice |

## Round discipline

- **Round 1:** the specific dispute (letters 01–07 as applicable).
- **Bureau says "verified":** escalate with letter 08 (method of verification) and/or re-dispute with **new** evidence — never resend the identical letter; bureaus may treat substantially identical disputes as frivolous (§611(a)(3)).
- **Item deleted, then back:** letter 10.
- Every round is logged; every response letter from a bureau is read in full and filed before the next round is planned.
