# Letter 05 — Late Payment Dispute

**When to use:** A payment is reported late for a month the client actually paid on time, the severity is wrong (30 days reported for a payment a few days late), or the late status/dates don't match the account history. Grounds: FCRA §611 (bureau reinvestigation) and §623 (the furnisher's duty to report accurately and to investigate direct notices of dispute).
**Uso:** Cuando un pago aparece tarde pero el cliente pagó a tiempo, o la fecha/gravedad está mal reportada.

**Closing checklist**
- [ ] Enclosures: government photo ID; proof of address; payment evidence (bank record or creditor confirmation showing date/amount)
- [ ] The claimed payment date in the letter matches the enclosed evidence exactly
- [ ] If the creditor supplied the error and the bureau verifies it, follow with a direct §623(a)(8) dispute to the furnisher
- [ ] Sent certified mail, return receipt requested; mailing date and ~30-day deadline logged
- [ ] Copy given to the client
