# Letter 02 — General Inaccuracy Dispute (§611)

**When to use:** A tradeline reports a specific, factual error that does not fit the collection, late-payment, or charge-off letters — wrong balance, wrong limit, wrong status, wrong dates, an account reported twice, or an account that is not the client's. State **one** clear inaccuracy per item, with the correct fact.
**Uso:** Para cualquier error específico en una cuenta que no encaje en las demás cartas. Un error claro por ítem, con el dato correcto.

**Closing checklist**
- [ ] Enclosures: copy of government photo ID; proof of address; the supporting evidence named in the letter
- [ ] The inaccuracy is specific and factual — not a blanket "not mine" without basis
- [ ] One dispute reason stated for this item
- [ ] Sent certified mail, return receipt requested; mailing date and ~30-day deadline logged
- [ ] Copy given to the client
