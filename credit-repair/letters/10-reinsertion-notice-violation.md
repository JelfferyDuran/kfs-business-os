# Letter 10 — Reinsertion Notice Violation

**When to use:** An item that was **deleted** after a dispute has **reappeared** on the report, and the bureau did not send the required written notice before reinserting it. FCRA §611(a)(5)(B) (15 U.S.C. §1681i(a)(5)(B)) permits reinsertion only if the furnisher certifies the information is complete and accurate, and requires the bureau to notify the consumer **in writing, within 5 business days of the reinsertion**, including a statement of the consumer's rights. No notice = the item must come back off.
**Uso:** Cuando un ítem eliminado regresa al reporte sin el aviso escrito que exige §611(a)(5)(B). Sin aviso, deben quitarlo de nuevo.

**Re: Unlawful reinsertion of deleted information without required notice — FCRA §611(a)(5)(B) (15 U.S.C. §1681i(a)(5)(B))**

Dear Sir or Madam:

I am writing regarding information that was deleted from my credit file following a dispute and has since been reinserted without the notice the FCRA requires.

**The history, from your own records:**

- On {{original_dispute_date}}, I disputed the following item: **{{furnisher_name}}, account {{account_number_partial}} — {{item_description}}**.
- By results notice dated {{deletion_notice_date}}, you confirmed the item was **deleted** from my report.
- On my report dated {{new_report_date}} (report/confirmation number {{new_report_number}}), the same item has reappeared.

Before deleted information may be reinserted, §611(a)(5)(B) requires that the furnisher certify the information is complete and accurate, and that you notify me **in writing not later than 5 business days after the reinsertion**, together with a statement of my right to dispute the information and to add a statement to my file. I received **no such notice** — not before the reinsertion, and not within 5 business days after it.

Because the statutory condition for reinsertion was not met, I request that you **delete this item again immediately** and confirm the deletion in writing. If you contend notice was sent, please provide a copy of the notice and the date and address to which it was mailed, together with the furnisher's certification you relied upon.

Please also send me an updated copy of my report reflecting the deletion. I have retained copies of my original dispute, your deletion notice, and the report showing the reinsertion, and I will pursue the remedies the FCRA provides if this is not corrected.

Sincerely,

**Closing checklist**
- [ ] Enclosures: government photo ID; proof of address; copy of the bureau's original deletion notice; copy of the report page showing the item reinserted
- [ ] Deletion date and reinsertion report date verified from actual documents before sending — the timeline is the violation
- [ ] Client confirms no reinsertion notice arrived by mail (check the full 5-business-day window after the reinsertion date)
- [ ] Sent certified mail, return receipt requested; dates logged
- [ ] If the bureau refuses or produces only a defective notice: escalate — CFPB complaint and attorney referral are the next steps; document everything for counsel
- [ ] Copy given to the client
